Game Rules

Goal: Reach 20 Autonomy Points (AP) to win!

Turn Phases:

  1. Main Phase: Play Policies, Technologies, or Biospheres from your hand. Place Energy Sources into your Grid (Max 4 slots).
  2. Activate Phase: Click Energy Sources in your Grid to activate them. Decide to convert the energy into AP (Victory Points) or Buy Energy (to spend in the Market).
  3. Buy Phase: Buy cards from the Market using your Buy Energy pool.
  4. Export Phase: Offer any unspent Buy Energy to your opponent. If they accept, they get the energy for their next turn, and you gain AP! Thermal energy sources are discarded, while Renewables can be kept in the grid. Draw 4 cards for the next turn.

End Game Conditions:

  • Win: A player reaches 20 AP.
  • Biosphere Collapse: If a player trashes all 4 of their Biospheres, they suffer a -8 AP penalty and the game ends immediately. The player with the highest AP wins.
  • Climate Collapse: If the Global CO2 track reaches 40, the game ends. Players subtract 1 AP for every card in their deck/hand/grid that produces CO2. The highest AP wins.

Catastrophes: At 10, 20, 30, and 40 CO2, a randomized catastrophe triggers, forcing players to discard hands, grid cards, or trash biospheres!
